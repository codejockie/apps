How can we help?

Welcome to the support page for Mina. If you're experiencing any issues, have questions, or would like to suggest new features, we'd love to hear from you.

🎵 Audio Problems

If subject audio isn't playing, ensure the audio has been downloaded or you are connected to the internet and your device isn't muted.

📖 Missing Content

If content appears missing, refresh cache or update to the latest version available in the app store.

💡 Suggestions

We continually improve the app. Send us your ideas and feature requests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an internet connection required? +

Yes, an internet connection is required to sync with the server.

How do I report a bug? +

Please send us an email describing the issue, your device model, operating system version, and steps to reproduce it.

Can I request a feature? +

Yes. We welcome suggestions for features and improvements. While we can't guarantee every request, we review all feedback.
